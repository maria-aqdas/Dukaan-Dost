How it works

Code forecasts

Each product gets a weekday pattern (Fridays and weekends sell more) and a smoothed level. The forecast for a day is level times that weekday's factor.

Code decides

Safety stock covers normal ups and downs. If stock will not last until a delivery could arrive, the product is flagged and a quantity is suggested.

AI explains

An open model on AMD Token Factory writes the explanation and the supplier message. A checker rejects any draft with numbers that are not in the data.

What this does not do

The sample shop is synthetic, so the 28-day test shows the method works on realistic data, not that it will save your shop a specific amount. Test it on your own ledger before relying on it.

It does not know about festivals, price changes, or shortages at your supplier. Salary-week and Ramadan jumps will be under-forecast at first.

Fewer stockouts usually means holding more stock. The test shows both, so you can judge the trade-off.
